# Evidence: Code of Conduct Acknowledgment (CC1.1-01)

**Control Reference:** CC1.1-01
**Evidence Type:** Policy document + acknowledgment tracking log

## Summary
All NorthLedger employees are required to review and electronically acknowledge 
the Code of Conduct and Ethics Policy upon hire and annually thereafter.

## Evidence Details
- Policy document version: v3.2 (last updated November 2024)
- Acknowledgment tracked via HRIS platform (BambooHR)
- Sample acknowledgment log (simulated):

| Employee ID | Department | Acknowledgment Date |
|---|---|---|
| EMP-0231 | Engineering | 2025-01-14 |
| EMP-0198 | Finance | 2025-01-15 |
| EMP-0276 | Customer Success | 2025-01-16 |

## Completion Rate
97% of active employees acknowledged within 30 days of policy release. 
Remaining 3% (new hires onboarded mid-cycle) completed acknowledgment 
within their individual onboarding window.

## Auditor Note
This evidence satisfies CC1.1 by demonstrating a documented, trackable 
process for communicating organizational conduct expectations.
