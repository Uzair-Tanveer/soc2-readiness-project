# Evidence: Organizational Chart (CC1.4-01)

**Control Reference:** CC1.4-01
**Evidence Type:** Organizational chart document
**Status:** ⚠️ Partially Implemented — see gap note below

## Summary
An organizational chart exists defining reporting lines across Engineering, 
IT, Security, Finance, and Customer Success functions.

## Evidence Details
- Document location: Internal wiki, "Company Structure" page
- Last reviewed/updated: **September 2024**
- Current date of assessment: June 2025

## Gap Identified
The organizational chart has not been formally reviewed or re-approved in 
9 months, despite two role changes (promotion of a Senior Engineer to 
Engineering Manager, and a new IT Manager hire) that are not yet reflected.

## Auditor Note
This control is marked **Partially Implemented** because while a chart 
exists, its accuracy cannot be relied upon without a recent review. 
See `docs/02-gap-analysis.md`, Gap 1, for remediation plan.
