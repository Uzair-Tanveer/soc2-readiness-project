# Evidence: Quarterly User Access Review — Q2 (CC6.2-01)

**Control Reference:** CC6.2-01
**Evidence Type:** Access review tracking log
**Status:** ⚠️ Partially Implemented — see gap note below

## Summary
Quarterly access reviews validate that employee access aligns with current 
job function (least privilege principle).

## Evidence Details

| Quarter | Scheduled Date | Completed Date | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 2025 | March 31, 2025 | March 28, 2025 | ✅ On Time |
| Q2 2025 | June 30, 2025 | *Not yet completed* | ⚠️ Overdue (3 weeks) |

## Gap Identified
The Q2 2025 access review was due June 30, 2025 but has not been completed 
as of the assessment date. This breaks the control's stated quarterly 
cadence and creates a window where access changes (role changes, 
terminations) may not have been validated.

## Auditor Note
This control is marked **Partially Implemented** because while Q1 was 
completed on schedule, the missed Q2 review indicates inconsistent 
execution. See `docs/02-gap-analysis.md`, Gap 6, for remediation plan.
