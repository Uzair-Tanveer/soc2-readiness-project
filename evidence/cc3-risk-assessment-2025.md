# Evidence: Annual Risk Assessment (CC3.1-01)

**Control Reference:** CC3.1-01
**Evidence Type:** Risk assessment report

## Summary
NorthLedger completed its formal annual enterprise risk assessment in 
Q1 2025, covering security, availability, and confidentiality risk domains.

## Evidence Details
- Assessment conducted: February 3–14, 2025
- Facilitated by: CISO, with participation from Engineering, IT, and Legal
- Methodology: Qualitative risk scoring (Likelihood x Impact, 1–5 scale)

## Top Risks Identified (Sample Excerpt)

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Score | Mitigation Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Unauthorized access via compromised credentials | 3 | 5 | 15 | IT Manager |
| Extended outage due to untested DR failover | 3 | 4 | 12 | Engineering Manager |
| Third-party subprocessor data breach | 2 | 5 | 10 | CISO |

## Outcome
Risk register (see `evidence/cc3-risk-register.md`) was updated based on 
this assessment. Results were presented to executive leadership on 
February 20, 2025.

## Auditor Note
This evidence satisfies CC3.1 by demonstrating a structured, repeatable 
risk identification process conducted within the assessment period.
